# Northbridge Hospital Operations Policies

## Staffing and Scheduling

### Workforce Coverage

Managers schedule qualified staff to meet anticipated service needs, required skills, and patient or operational demand. Staffing gaps, unsafe workloads, and unplanned absences are escalated through the approved coverage process. Temporary reassignment does not exceed a staff member's verified competence or authorization.

*SEC_011*

### Rosters and Time Records

Work schedules, on-call arrangements, leave, and time records are maintained in the authorized system. Staff review assigned shifts and report discrepancies promptly. Managers approve changes, overtime, and agency use in accordance with workforce, budget, and fatigue-management requirements.

*SEC_012*

## Facilities and Environment

### Safe Facilities

Facilities are maintained to support safe, accessible, and reliable services. Staff report hazards, damaged infrastructure, utility disruptions, and blocked emergency routes without delay. The responsible facilities team assesses risk, implements interim controls, and coordinates repairs or service continuity measures.

*SEC_013*

### Environmental Cleanliness

Departments maintain clean, orderly work areas and follow approved cleaning, waste, and spill-response procedures. Staff restrict access to contaminated or unsafe areas until they are made safe. Cleaning concerns that may affect patients, staff, or visitors are reported to Environmental Services or the responsible manager.

*SEC_014*

## Finance and Procurement

### Authorized Purchasing

Goods and services are purchased only through approved procurement channels and within delegated financial authority. Staff document the business need, obtain required approvals, and select suppliers using applicable purchasing requirements. Personal purchases, conflicts of interest, and unauthorized commitments are prohibited.

*SEC_015*

### Asset and Supply Stewardship

Departments safeguard supplies, equipment, and other organizational assets. Inventory is used responsibly, stored securely, and monitored for expiry, loss, or unusual consumption. Missing, damaged, or defective assets are reported promptly so that replacement, repair, and financial controls can be applied.

*SEC_016*

## Quality and Improvement

### Quality Monitoring

Leaders monitor service quality through defined measures, audits, feedback, and operational review. Results are used to identify variation, risks, and improvement opportunities. Teams document actions, assign accountable owners, and review progress until the intended improvement is achieved or the approach is revised.

*SEC_017*

### Change Management

Operational changes that may affect service delivery, safety, staffing, systems, or compliance are assessed before implementation. The change owner defines scope, risks, approvals, communication, training, and contingency arrangements. Material changes are reviewed after implementation to confirm that expected outcomes and controls remain effective.

*SEC_018*

## Incident Management

### Operational Incidents

Staff report operational incidents, including service disruptions, equipment failures, security concerns, and hazards, through the approved process. Immediate actions focus on safety, continuity, and preservation of relevant facts. Managers coordinate escalation, communication, and recovery according to the incident's severity and impact.

*SEC_019*

### Review and Corrective Action

Significant incidents are reviewed to identify contributing factors, control failures, and lessons learned. Corrective actions specify an owner, due date, and method for verifying completion. Findings are shared with affected teams at an appropriate level of detail to strengthen prevention and operational resilience.

*SEC_020*
