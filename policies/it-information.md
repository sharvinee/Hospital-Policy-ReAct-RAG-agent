# Northbridge Hospital IT and Information Governance Policies

## Cybersecurity and Access Control

### Secure Access

Access to systems, applications, and data is granted only after identity verification, business approval, and assignment of the minimum permissions needed for the role. Shared accounts are prohibited unless formally approved. Privileged access receives additional controls, logging, and periodic review.

*SEC_021*

### Authentication and Credentials

Users protect passwords, multi-factor authentication methods, tokens, and other credentials from disclosure or misuse. Credentials must not be shared, stored insecurely, or reused contrary to approved standards. Suspected credential compromise is reported immediately so access can be reset, investigated, and protected.

*SEC_022*

## Acceptable Use and Systems Management

### Acceptable Technology Use

Organizational systems, networks, devices, and software are used for authorized business purposes and in accordance with law and policy. Users must not install unapproved software, bypass security controls, or use systems to access, create, or distribute inappropriate content. Limited personal use is permitted only where approved.

*SEC_023*

### Managed Systems and Devices

IT manages supported systems and devices through approved configuration, patching, inventory, and endpoint protection processes. Users do not alter security settings or connect unauthorized devices. Lost, stolen, malfunctioning, or suspected-compromised equipment is reported promptly to enable containment and recovery.

*SEC_024*

## Data Governance and Retention

### Data Classification and Handling

Information is classified and handled according to its sensitivity, legal obligations, and business value. Restricted and confidential data is shared only with authorized recipients through approved methods. Staff use approved storage, transfer, and disposal processes to prevent unauthorized access, loss, or alteration.

*SEC_025*

### Data Retention and Disposal

Records and data are retained for approved business, clinical, legal, and regulatory periods. When retention ends, information is securely deleted, destroyed, or archived through authorized processes. Legal holds, investigations, or audits suspend routine disposal until the responsible authority releases the information.

*SEC_026*

## Security Incidents and Business Continuity

### Information Security Incidents

Suspected phishing, malware, unauthorized access, data exposure, or other security incidents are reported immediately through the approved channel. Users preserve relevant information and do not investigate beyond their authority. IT and security teams assess impact, contain the incident, coordinate communications, and document remediation.

*SEC_027*

### Business Continuity and Recovery

Critical services maintain documented continuity and recovery arrangements for outages, cyber incidents, and other disruptions. Plans identify essential functions, responsible roles, communication routes, backup methods, and recovery priorities. Teams test plans periodically and update them after exercises, incidents, or material system changes.

*SEC_028*
