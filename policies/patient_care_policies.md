# Northbridge Hospital Patient Care Policies

## Clinical Care and Patient Safety

### Safe Clinical Care

Clinicians provide care within their role, competence, and approved clinical procedures. Before treatment, they confirm the patient's identity, relevant history, allergies, current condition, and care plan. Concerns about deterioration, unclear instructions, or unsafe conditions are escalated promptly to the responsible clinician or supervisor.

*SEC_001*

### Safety Events and Near Misses

Staff report safety events, near misses, and hazardous conditions through the approved reporting process as soon as practical. Reports must be factual, timely, and free of blame. The responsible team reviews events, addresses immediate risks, and uses findings to improve patient safety.

*SEC_002*

## Privacy and Confidentiality

### Protection of Patient Information

Patient information is accessed, used, and shared only when necessary for authorized care, operations, or legal obligations. Staff protect records from unauthorized viewing, discussion, disclosure, loss, or alteration. Conversations and screens containing patient information must not be visible or audible to unauthorized people.

*SEC_003*

### Privacy Incidents

Suspected misdirected messages, inappropriate access, lost records, or other privacy incidents are reported immediately through the approved incident process. Staff preserve relevant information and do not attempt to conceal or independently resolve the incident. Privacy and leadership teams assess the event and coordinate follow-up.

*SEC_004*

## Consent and Patient Rights

### Informed Consent

Before a procedure or treatment requiring consent, the responsible clinician explains its purpose, expected benefits, material risks, alternatives, and the option to decline. Consent is voluntary, documented in the health record, and confirmed before the intervention. Patients may ask questions or withdraw consent at any time.

*SEC_005*

### Respectful and Equitable Care

Patients receive respectful care that considers their dignity, values, culture, communication needs, and personal preferences. Staff arrange appropriate communication support when needed and involve approved support persons where possible. Discrimination, harassment, or retaliation against patients or families is prohibited.

*SEC_006*

## Clinical Documentation

### Accurate Health Records

Clinical documentation is timely, accurate, objective, and sufficient to support safe continuity of care. Entries identify the author, record relevant assessments, decisions, interventions, and outcomes, and follow approved recordkeeping standards. Staff correct errors through the authorized amendment process without obscuring the original record.

*SEC_007*

### Record Access and Corrections

Patients may request access to their medical records or ask for corrections through the designated health information process. Requests are verified, documented, and handled within applicable requirements. Staff do not alter records in response to a request unless the authorized review determines that an amendment is appropriate.

*SEC_008*

## Complaints and Feedback

### Patient Complaints and Grievances

Patients and representatives may raise concerns about care, service, communication, or rights without affecting their treatment. Staff listen respectfully, address immediate concerns when possible, and route formal grievances through the approved process. Complaints are documented, reviewed, and answered by the responsible team.

*SEC_009*

### Learning From Feedback

Leaders review complaint and feedback trends to identify recurring issues, risks, and opportunities to improve care. Improvement actions have an accountable owner, defined follow-up, and documented outcomes. Patients receive appropriate information about how their concern was handled, subject to privacy and legal requirements.

*SEC_010*
